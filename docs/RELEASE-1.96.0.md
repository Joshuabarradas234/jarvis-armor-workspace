## JARVIS 1.96.0: JARVIS for Mac

## New

- **JARVIS runs on a Mac with an Apple chip, such as your Mac mini.** Each release now has a second download, `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.96.0-mac.zip`.
  - On the Mac he runs his brain round the clock: the phone and WhatsApp, email, approvals, schedules and overnight jobs, reports, the Tower, Ask JARVIS and the think room.
  - The Mac stays awake while JARVIS runs (the screen can still sleep), so jobs never miss because the computer was asleep.
  - With a screen attached, the hall opens there too. Without one, he runs quietly in the menu bar (look for the word **JARVIS** at the top right).
- **Move JARVIS to another computer** (Settings → System). One file carries everything he knows from this PC to the Mac:
  - your suits, to-dos, ideas, the Tower, the calendar, reports, conversations, think room boards, and your standing orders and notes;
  - project folders are found again on the Mac, in OneDrive or on the Desktop or in Documents.

  Passwords and keys stay behind; you type them in again on the Mac. Whatever the move replaces on the Mac is kept.
- **Not on the Mac yet:** listening and speaking, the live wallpaper, arranging windows, hand control, and spotting calls to record. They come in a later version. Signing in to the Mac from your ZenBook comes in 1.97.

## Set up the Mac mini

1. On the Mac, download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.96.0-mac.zip` below. Double-click it, then drag **JARVIS Armor Workspace** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Allow JARVIS to use your **Documents** and **Desktop** folders when asked.
4. Click **JARVIS** in the menu bar, open **Settings → General** and tick **Start when the Mac starts**.

## Move JARVIS from the PC to the Mac (whenever you're ready)

1. On the PC, open **Settings → System → Move JARVIS to another computer** and click **Save everything to move…**. Leave **Switch off JARVIS Core here** ticked, so only the Mac runs the brain and he never phones you twice. A file called `JARVIS move <date>.json` goes on your Desktop.
2. Copy that file to the Mac, using OneDrive or a USB stick.
3. On the Mac, open **Settings → System → Bring JARVIS over…**, choose the file, check what it says, and click **Bring it over**. JARVIS restarts with everything.
4. On the Mac, open **JARVIS Core** and add your keys again: Claude, Twilio, the Gmail app password and AssemblyAI.

## Install on Windows

Click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS, or **INSTALL JARVIS 1.96.0** in the tray menu.

Or by hand:

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.96.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_96_0`.
