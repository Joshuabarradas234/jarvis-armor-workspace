## JARVIS 1.105.0: the full weekly report on both computers

## Fixed

- **The weekly report is complete again, on the Mac mini and the ZenBook.** It plays every Sunday after 5pm, and whenever you say "Jarvis, weekly review". Since JARVIS's brain moved to the Mac, the report on each computer was missing:
  - the meetings kept on the Mac;
  - what the AI cost;
  - the dates ahead;
  - how many emails came in.

  Now both computers fetch those from the Mac, so the report covers your whole week wherever you watch it.

## On the Mac mini

Click **Install now**. It updates the Mac app and JARVIS Server together, which this update needs.

If the Mac is still on 1.102.0 or earlier, install 1.104.0 or later from the releases page by hand first. After that, **Install now** works.

## Install on Windows

Click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS, or **INSTALL JARVIS 1.105.0** in the tray menu.

Or by hand:

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.105.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_105_0`.
