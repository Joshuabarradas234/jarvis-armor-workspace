## JARVIS 1.93.1: calmer overnight reports

## Fixed

- **The overnight report no longer leads with "I couldn't reach your mail server" or "I couldn't reach Twilio".** Those happen when the PC is asleep or the Wi-Fi is off, which is normal. They now get one short line at the end of the report. Real problems, such as a refused password, are still reported as before.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.93.1-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Or click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS once 1.93.0 is installed.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_93_1`.
