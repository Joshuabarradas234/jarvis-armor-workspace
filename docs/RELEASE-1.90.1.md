## JARVIS 1.90.1: look at my screen, in any app

## Better

- **"Look at my screen" works wherever you are.** In Chrome, Word, a game or anything else, say "Jarvis, look at my screen" or press **Ctrl+Alt+L**. It works even when JARVIS is closed to the tray, and it doesn't open the hall.
- **His answer floats on top.** It appears in a small card in the corner of the screen you're using, above every app, without taking the keyboard from what you're doing. Drag the card by its title to move it. Close it with **×** or **Esc**.
- **Keep asking.** While the card is open, just say your next question ("how do I fix it?"), with or without his name. You can also type it in the card. **Look again** takes a new picture, and the card hides itself first so it isn't in the picture.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.90.1-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_90_1`.
