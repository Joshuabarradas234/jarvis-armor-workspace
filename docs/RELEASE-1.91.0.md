## JARVIS 1.91.0: meeting co-pilot and hand control

## New

- **A live meeting co-pilot on your lower screen.** When you record a meeting, a panel opens on the lower screen. It shows:
  - **things you could say next**, at the top;
  - **running notes**: what the meeting is about, what was agreed, who does what and by when, and questions nobody has answered yet;
  - **the transcript** as it comes in.

  Type a question about the meeting so far ("who is sending the price list?") and he answers on the panel. Say "Jarvis, meeting notes" to update the notes straight away. He shows them rather than saying them, so nobody on your call hears him. Pages on the lower screen move aside to make room for the panel. The notes use Claude's quick model, at most $1 per meeting. The summary and follow-up email draft at the end are written as before.
- **Iron Man hand control for your windows.** Say "Jarvis, hand control on", or tick **Hand control when closed** in the tray menu. Then, whenever JARVIS is closed to the tray:
  - your hand moves a glowing ring over your screens;
  - **make a fist** over a window to pick it up, and move your hand to carry it;
  - **open your hand** to put it down, or **throw it** towards another screen to send it there (a maximised window is maximised again where it lands);
  - **pinch and hold** for a second to open JARVIS. In the hall, pinch a suit to open it, as before.

  It only moves windows: it never clicks, types or closes anything. The camera is on while it runs, and it stops when you open JARVIS or lock the PC. Say "Jarvis, hand control off" (this works from the tray too) or untick it in the tray menu. "Calibrate hands" in the hall also sets where your hand needs to be for this.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.91.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_91_0`.
