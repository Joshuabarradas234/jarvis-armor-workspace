## JARVIS 1.106.1: long Tower work no longer cut off

## Fixed

- **A floor's long pieces of work are finished in full.** Floor 30's first job (13 posts for Panorama, each with its picture brief) was good, but Gatekeeper's final write-up ran past the length limit, so JARVIS marked it "needs changes". Now:
  - the final reviewer can write twice as much;
  - each team member can write more than before;
  - JARVIS waits longer for a long answer to arrive.

  A piece that still runs too long is caught as before, and never passed off as finished.

## Install

Click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS, or **INSTALL JARVIS 1.106.1** in the tray menu. On the Mac mini, click **Install now** as well.

Or, on Windows, by hand:

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.106.1-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_106_1`.
