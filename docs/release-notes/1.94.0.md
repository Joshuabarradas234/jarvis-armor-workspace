## JARVIS 1.94.0: Ask JARVIS anywhere, and projects in their suits

## New

- **Ask JARVIS, from anywhere.** Press **Ctrl + Alt + Space** in any app, or say "Jarvis, I've got a question", and a JARVIS window opens. You can also use **Ask JARVIS** in the tray menu, or right-click JARVIS on the taskbar.
  - Pick a project along the top, or **Everything**, and ask how it's going, what's next or what you decided last time. JARVIS reads the project's files list, its README and its notes, plus your to-dos, the Tower's jobs, your ideas and your calendar.
  - Switch to **Think together** to brainstorm. JARVIS builds on your idea, gives you two or three options with their pros and cons, asks a sharp question or two, and suggests the next step. Say "Jarvis, let's think about the summer menu" to start straight away.
  - **Each project keeps its own conversation, and it never runs out.** Older messages are folded into a short summary.
  - **Save to project notes** writes what you decided into a `JARVIS notes.md` file in that project's folder. JARVIS reads it every time you ask about that project again, so nothing is lost when a conversation ends.
  - **Start building** turns the conversation into an idea card for that suit in the Ideas room, ready for **Plan**.
  - **Read aloud** and **Copy** sit under each answer. The pin keeps the window on top of other windows.
- **Its own taskbar icon.** While the Ask JARVIS window is open, right-click its icon on the taskbar and choose **Pin to taskbar**. After that, one click opens it, even when JARVIS is closed.
- **Choose your own key.** Settings → General → Keyboard shortcuts → **Ask JARVIS**. Any key works, including a spare key on your keyboard such as F13.
- **Your Desktop project folders, filed into suits, and you decide where.** When there are folders on your Desktop that no suit holds, a card in the hall asks about each one: **is it for your company, family business or personal?** The card shows your own names; the company name comes from the Tower. Then it asks **which suit**.
  - The folder moves into the suit (Documents › JARVIS Workspaces › the hall). Untick **Move it off the Desktop** to leave it where it is and just link it.
  - **Put it back** undoes it, and **Show me** opens it in File Explorer.
  - **Not a project** stops JARVIS asking about that folder, and **Ask me next week** waits a week.
  - The card appears by itself at most once a day. Say "Jarvis, tidy my desktop" or choose **File desktop projects** in the tray to open it whenever you like.
  - Nothing moves while a folder is open in an editor, a terminal or File Explorer: JARVIS asks you to close it first. Your JARVIS source folder is never offered.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.94.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Or click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS once 1.93.0 is installed.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_94_0`.
