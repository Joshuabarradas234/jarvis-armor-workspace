## JARVIS 1.97.1: JARVIS Link appears in Settings

## Fixed

- **Settings → System now shows JARVIS Link**, with **Find my Mac**, the pairing code and **Sign in**. After you sign in, it shows **Move JARVIS's brain to the Mac** and **Sign out**. **Move JARVIS by file** appears there too.
  - In 1.97.0 these sections were added to an old copy of the Settings page that JARVIS no longer uses, so they never appeared. Everything behind them was already installed and working.

## Install

Click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS, or **INSTALL JARVIS 1.97.1** in the tray menu.

Or by hand:

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.97.1-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_97_1`.

JARVIS Server on the Mac mini doesn't need updating for this.
