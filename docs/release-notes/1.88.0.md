## JARVIS 1.88.0: brainstorm and plan projects, wake for calls

## New

- **Brainstorm projects with JARVIS.** In the Ideas room, press **💡 Brainstorm projects** and say what you are thinking about, for example "a side business I could run at weekends".
  - JARVIS suggests six projects that fit what you tell him about your time, money and skills. Each comes with why it could work, a first step, and a rough cost and time.
  - He asks up to three questions. Answer them and press **Go again** for sharper ideas, or press **More ideas like these**.
  - Add the ones you like to the room, or press **Add and plan it**. Your last ten brainstorms are kept.
- **Plan any idea step by step.** Open an idea and press **📋 Plan it out step by step**. JARVIS writes:
  - the goal, and what "finished" looks like;
  - the phases in order, with small steps in each;
  - who does each step: you, JARVIS (research or drafts for you to check), or the Tower's agents;
  - rough time and cost, what could go wrong and how to avoid it, what you will need, and what to do this week.
- **"How do I do this?"** on any step gives you a detailed walkthrough: what to prepare, the actions in order, mistakes to avoid, and how to know it is done.
- **Tick steps off** as you go. The idea's progress follows the plan. Copy a step, or the next phase's steps, into your to-dos. **Rethink the plan** rewrites it from your feedback and keeps the steps you have already done.
- **Waking for your calls.** If the PC is asleep, Windows now wakes it two minutes before JARVIS's next call, message or report, so they go out on time.
  - JARVIS keeps one Windows task called **JARVIS wake-up**, moves it when your schedule changes, and removes it when you quit him.
  - **Settings → JARVIS Core → Quiet hours and nights** shows the next wake time and whether Windows allows wake timers. On this PC that is when plugged in, not on battery.

## Good to know

- Brainstorms and plans only write text. Nothing is sent, bought or started.
- They use your Claude API key, or Claude Code if there is no key. A brainstorm or plan costs a few pence, which counts in JARVIS's daily spend.
- The **✦ Open Claude** button (it used to be called "Brainstorm with Claude") still opens Claude's website in a side panel.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.88.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_88_0`.
