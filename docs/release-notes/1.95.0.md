## JARVIS 1.95.0: the think room

## New

- **The think room: all your screens become one room for a project.** Say "Jarvis, open the think room" (or "Jarvis, let's build"). You can also press Ctrl + K and choose **Think room**, use **Think room** in the tray, or click the room button in Ask JARVIS. If you're in a suit, the room is for that suit.
  - **The board.** Four columns: **Ideas**, **Questions**, **Decided** and **Next steps**. Press **+** to add a card, drag cards between columns, and click **Edit** to change one. Next steps can be ticked **Done** or sent to your **to-dos**.
  - **JARVIS sits beside the board** to think with you. He sees the board as he answers. **Pin to board** turns his answer into cards, filed under the right column. **Sort the conversation onto the board** does that for the whole conversation.
  - **Every screen has its own tabs.** Type an address or a search in the box on that screen. **Drag a tab off one screen and let go over another** to move it there, or use the small screen numbers on the tab. The page moves as it is, without reloading. The board is a tab too, so you can put it on whichever screen you like, the TV included.
  - **The Screens buttons** (top right) add a screen to the room or give it back to Windows.
  - **Everything is kept for the project:** the board, which page was on which screen, and the conversation. It's the same conversation as in Ask JARVIS. The first time you open the room for a suit, it brings that suit's pages.
  - **Leave the room** (or "Jarvis, leave the room") saves the board into the project's folder as `JARVIS board.md`. **Save to project** does that any time. JARVIS reads the board whenever you ask about that project.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.95.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Or click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS once 1.93.0 is installed.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_95_0`.
