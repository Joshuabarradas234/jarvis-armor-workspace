## JARVIS 1.81.2: Batmobile hologram and live suit progress

## What's new

- Your supplied Batmobile is now a real, rotating cyan 3D hologram on the centre Batcave platform, with its full proportions and detail preserved.
- Each working suit shows a moving loading bar immediately, then its agent's reported percentage; queued, finished and blocked jobs are clearly labelled.
- The Batmobile pauses when you enter a workstation, cover the hall or turn animations off, and resumes when you return.
- This release also includes the 1.81.1 improvements: brighter Iron Man eyes and reactor power-up, Batman and Spider-Man activation effects, smoother glass opening sounds and larger centre displays.

## Fixed

- The centre Batmobile was still a video; the newly supplied 3D model was never connected to it.
- Progress styling could hide or shrink the loading indicators, especially before an agent reported a percentage.
- Restarting a finished agent now resets its old percentage and completed tasks.
- The last line an agent prints is now processed even without a final line break; stopped or blocked work is not automatically marked 100%.
- Board changes reach the hall immediately, and switching halls cannot apply a late progress response to the wrong hall.
- Included from 1.81.1: entry animations now work on battery, the first opening sound waits for audio readiness, and rigid suit movement avoids stretching from guessed skeletons.

## Install

1. Right-click the JARVIS tray icon → Quit.
2. Unzip JARVIS-1.81.2-update.zip into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\JARVIS Armor Workspace\resources and choose Replace.
3. Start JARVIS.

This drop-in update works over v1.80.1, v1.81.0 or v1.81.1. It includes the approved halls and the new Batmobile model; keep the existing suit models, vendor libraries and videos in place. The source ZIP is a development backup, not the installation file. The 65.8 MB Batmobile stays outside app.asar in assets/vehicles/.

## Undo

Use the tray → RESTART WITHOUT SELF-UPDATES, or reinstall the v1.80.1 zip. The tray option bypasses approved self-updates; reinstall the previous update ZIP to undo this base release. You can also reinstall the v1.81.0 or v1.81.1 update ZIP.
