## JARVIS 1.92.0: a Bible verse in every hall

## New

- **1 Corinthians 13:4–8 in every hall.** A small, faint label ("✝ 1 Cor 13:4–8") sits in the bottom-left corner of the Armour Hall, the Batcave and the Web Lab. Hover over it and it brightens. Click it (or pinch it with your hand) and it turns over to show the verse, ending "Love never fails." Click again to turn it back. It shows in the hall only, not inside a suit. The words are from the World English Bible, which is free to use.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.92.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_92_0`.
