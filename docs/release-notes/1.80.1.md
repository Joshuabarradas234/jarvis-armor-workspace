## JARVIS 1.80.1: JARVIS Core

JARVIS now keeps working while you are away, and asks you before he does anything that matters.

**New**
- **Phone and WhatsApp:** wake-up calls, a 06:00 overnight report on weekdays, and WhatsApp messages you can reply to ("goodnight", "call me", "what needs me", "undo").
- **Approvals:** anything involving your business, other people, money or his own code waits for your OK. Reply `YES 12 K7M3` on WhatsApp, press Approve in JARVIS Core, or say "Jarvis, approve 12". Text messages can never approve anything.
- **Overnight work:** an audit at 02:30 and a self-review at 03:00.
- **Email:** sorts and labels your Gmail and drafts replies. Nothing is sent without your YES.
- **Self-updates:** JARVIS can improve his own code in a private copy. You see every changed line, and nothing is installed without your approval. Every file is checked at each start, and a version that fails to start rolls back by itself.
- Everything from 1.72.x is kept: meeting mode, the Ideas room, and Claude Code support.

**Install**
1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.80.1-update.zip` below.
2. Right-click it, choose **Extract All…**, open the folder and run **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**.
3. Open **Settings → JARVIS Core** and follow the setup: your mobile number, Twilio, the WhatsApp sandbox and a Claude API key. Full guide: [docs/JARVIS-CORE.md](https://github.com/Joshuabarradas234/jarvis-armor-workspace/blob/main/docs/JARVIS-CORE.md)

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_80_1`.
