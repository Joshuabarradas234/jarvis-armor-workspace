## JARVIS 1.104.0: the second screen's buttons

## New

- **Earth opens on the second screen.** Press **Earth** on the second screen and the globe opens right there, not on the main screen. "Jarvis, show me the earth" still opens it on the main screen.
- **Arrange brings your open tabs down and tidies them.** Press **Arrange** and every tab and floating page open on the main screen comes down to the second screen, laid out in a tidy grid (up to 12). **Send all up** sends them back.
- **The check-up shows memory.** **Settings → System → This computer** shows how much memory JARVIS is using, and what to do if the computer is short of it.

**Systems** already opens on the second screen: it shows the system readings and your suits, and **Back to the deck** returns.

## Putting files into a suit from the ZenBook

Open the suit in JARVIS on the ZenBook and drag files from your desktop into its **Files** panel. They're copied into the suit's folder, which is on the Mac mini (drive J:). Deskflow can't carry files between computers, only the mouse and keyboard.

## On the Mac mini

If you're on 1.103.0 or later, click **Install now**. Otherwise download **JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.104.0-mac-intel.dmg** from the releases page, quit JARVIS, drag it onto **Applications** and choose **Replace**.

## Install on Windows

Click **Install now** on the banner in JARVIS, or **INSTALL JARVIS 1.104.0** in the tray menu.

Or by hand:

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.104.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_104_0`.
