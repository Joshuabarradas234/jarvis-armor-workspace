## JARVIS 1.90.0: look at my screen, second brain, cinematic briefing

## New

- **"Jarvis, look at my screen."** JARVIS takes a picture of the screen you're working on (the one under the mouse), reads it, and answers out loud and on a card. Ask a question with it: "Jarvis, what does this error mean?", "Jarvis, look at my screen and tell me how to fix it", or press **Ctrl+Alt+L**. Ask follow-up questions on the card about the same picture for ten minutes, or press **Look again**. The picture stays in memory only and is never saved. JARVIS only looks when you ask on this PC, never because of a message.
- **Your second brain.** Ask about anything in your emails (all of Gmail, not just the inbox), Work desk documents, meeting notes, ideas, project plans, brainstorms, Tower results, JARVIS's notes, standing orders, to-dos, calendar, and bills and birthdays. JARVIS answers with numbered sources underneath. Click a number to see where it came from, and open meeting notes, Tower results and documents straight from the card. Say "Jarvis, what did we agree with the landlord?", "Jarvis, search everything for the boiler quote" or "Jarvis, what do I know about council tax?", or press **Ctrl+Alt+F**. It works from WhatsApp too.
- **A cinematic morning briefing.** The morning briefing now plays full screen, with film bars and one scene for each part of your day:
  - the date and the weather;
  - what the Tower finished overnight, and what's waiting for your OK;
  - email: how many came in, which need a reply, and which sound unhappy;
  - today's calendar as a timeline;
  - bills and birthdays coming up;
  - your to-dos and how far your projects are.

  Each scene appears as JARVIS starts saying it. A quiet day gives a short briefing. Press **Esc**, click, or press **Skip** to end it.
- **"Good morning, Jarvis"** now gives you the day's first briefing if you haven't had it yet, instead of just a greeting.

## Fixed

- **The Iron Man hall's hologram stands in the middle of the pad.** It stood a little to the left and towards the back. It now stands on the centre of the pad.
- After you approved or commented on a finished Tower job, JARVIS's memory of jobs he had already announced kept growing. It now keeps only the last 200, as intended.

## Install

1. Download `JARVIS-Armor-Workspace-1.90.0-update.zip` below, right-click it and choose **Extract All…**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **INSTALL-UPDATE.bat**. It closes JARVIS, keeps a copy of the old version, copies the update in and starts JARVIS again.

Your previous version is kept in `resources\backup-before-1_90_0`.
