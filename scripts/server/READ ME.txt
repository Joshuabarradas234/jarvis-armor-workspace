JARVIS Server
=============

JARVIS's brain, for a Mac that stays on: the phone and WhatsApp, email, approvals, schedules, overnight jobs,
reports, your to-dos and your calendar. It has no windows. It runs in the background and starts by itself
whenever the Mac starts. Your ZenBook signs in to it over Tailscale.

It needs:
  - Node.js 22  (nodejs.org/dist/latest-v22.x/, the .pkg). Newer versions don't run on macOS 12.
  - Tailscale, signed in with the same account as the ZenBook.

To install:
  1. Right-click "Install JARVIS Server" and choose Open, then Open again.
     (The first time, macOS asks because the file isn't from the App Store.)
  2. A window shows the pairing code. On the ZenBook, open JARVIS, then
     Settings > System > JARVIS Link, choose this Mac and type the code.

To pair another computer later, right-click "Pair a device" and choose Open.
To remove it, right-click "Uninstall JARVIS Server" and choose Open. Your data is kept.

Where things are:
  ~/Library/Application Support/JARVIS Server/data       JARVIS's data, and jarvis.log
  ~/Library/Application Support/JARVIS Server/Documents  standing orders and notes
